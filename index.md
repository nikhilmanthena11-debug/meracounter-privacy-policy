# MeraCounter — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 31, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

MeraCounter is a business management application designed to help businesses manage their daily operations, including inventory, customers, sales, services, invoices, and business records.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when using the MeraCounter application.

For privacy questions, contact: **nikhilmanthena11@gmail.com**

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, MeraCounter may process:

- **Account information:** Email address, user identifier, business-owner profile details, and account verification status.
- **Business information:** Business name, business category, contact details, and business settings.
- **Customer information:** Customer names, contact details, vehicle information, and service or purchase history entered by authorized business users.
- **Transaction information:** Sales records, invoices, payment methods recorded in the app, inventory transactions, and service records.
- **Images:** Optional product or other business-related photographs uploaded by users.
- **Technical information:** Authentication information, application security signals, and device or application information processed by integrated services.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information is processed to:

- Register and authenticate user accounts.
- Operate business-management features.
- Generate invoices and maintain business records.
- Manage employees and business permissions.
- Maintain business subscriptions and access settings.
- Protect accounts and prevent unauthorized access.
- Respond to support inquiries and account-deletion requests.

## 4. Data Storage and Service Providers

MeraCounter uses Google Firebase and associated Google Cloud services, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Functions.

These services process information as necessary to operate the application and provide authentication, data storage, and backend functionality.

Google's applicable privacy and security terms also govern its processing of information.

## 5. Business Data Separation

MeraCounter is designed to maintain separate records for individual businesses.

Access to business information is controlled through authentication, business membership, assigned permissions, and backend security checks.

Users are responsible for ensuring that customer and business information they enter is collected and used appropriately.

## 6. Information Sharing

Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate MeraCounter, including Google Firebase and Google Cloud.

Business information may also be accessed by authorized users of the relevant business according to their assigned permissions.

Additional disclosures may occur where legally required or necessary to protect the security of the service.

## 7. Data Security

MeraCounter uses security measures including Firebase Authentication, access controls, Firestore security rules, and server-side authorization.

No electronic service can guarantee absolute security, but we work to restrict unauthorized access and protect information handled through the application.

## 8. Data Retention

Account and business records are retained as necessary to provide the service and meet applicable legal, operational, security, and recordkeeping requirements.

Some invoice, financial, or transaction records may need to be retained for legitimate legal or regulatory reasons, even following an account-deletion request.

## 9. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their MeraCounter account using the available account-deletion request option in the application or by contacting **nikhilmanthena11@gmail.com**.

Deletion requests may require identity and business-ownership verification. We will review associated account and business data, delete eligible data, and identify any records that must be retained for lawful reasons.

Where information must be retained, it will be retained only as necessary for the relevant purpose.

## 10. Children's Privacy

MeraCounter is intended for business management and is not designed for children.

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to the application's features, data practices, or applicable requirements.
