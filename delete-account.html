MeraCounter - Account Deletion Request

You can request deletion of your MeraCounter account and associated personal data.

How to request deletion

Send an email from your registered MeraCounter email address with the subject: "MeraCounter Account Deletion Request".

Request Account Deletion

If you cannot access your registered email, contact support using another email address. We may need to verify your account ownership. Never send us your password.

What happens next?

We review your request and verify account ownership before processing deletion. If you own businesses with active records or employees, we may need to coordinate business ownership or record retention.

Data deletion and retention

Eligible account information and associated personal data will be deleted as part of the account deletion process.

Certain invoices, transaction records or security records may need to be retained for legitimate legal or regulatory reasons. Our Privacy Policy explains our data practices.
